Make a Difference

In Gambia, many are struggling to have access to clean hygienic water. 703 million people worldwide lack basic access to safe drinking water. In Tujering, families are affected daily by this shortage.

We study the needs of the community, identify the most suitable locations for boreholes, and ensure each project directly benefits the families who need it most.

To guarantee success, we coordinate with experienced suppliers like Waterpoint, who bring proven expertise and skill to every borehole project. Together we ensure that each water source is safe, sustainable, and long-lasting.

Your support can create life-changing impact. Every contribution helps provide clean water to families in need, transforming lives and communities.

About Us

The heart behind Nyodema Charity Foundation is Mr. Ousman Daffeh, a passionate and dedicated teacher who has served communities across The Gambia for over fifteen years. During his journey as an educator, moving from one region to another, he came face to face with the daily struggles of families in remote areas.
